Crispy on the outside, incredibly cheesy on the inside, and packed with savory garlic flavor. These Cheesy Garlic Chicken Wraps come together in minutes and satisfy every comfort food craving.

## Why You’ll Love This Recipe

- **Quick & Easy:** Perfect for a busy weeknight or a satisfying work-from-home lunch.
- **Kid-Friendly:** A guaranteed hit with the whole family thanks to the gooey cheese and familiar flavors.
- **Great for Leftovers:** An excellent way to use up leftover rotisserie or grilled chicken.

## Kitchen Tools You’ll Need

- Large skillet
- Cutting board and sharp knife
- Small mixing bowl
- Pastry brush

## Ingredients

- 4 large Flour Tortillas
- 2 cups Cooked Chicken (shredded or diced)
- 1.5 cups Mozzarella Cheese (shredded)
- 1/2 cup Parmesan Cheese (grated)
- 4 tbsp Unsalted Butter (melted)
- 3 cloves Garlic (minced)
- 1 tbsp Fresh Parsley (chopped)
- 1/2 tsp Garlic Powder

## Preparation

1. **Make Garlic Butter:** In a small bowl, mix the melted butter, minced garlic, and fresh chopped parsley.
2. **Season Chicken:** Toss the cooked chicken with the garlic powder and a pinch of salt and pepper to taste.
3. **Assemble Wraps:** Lay the tortillas flat. Sprinkle mozzarella and parmesan in the center, top with the seasoned chicken, and add a little more cheese. Fold the sides in and roll up tightly like a burrito.
4. **Toast the Wraps:** Heat a large skillet over medium heat. Brush the outside of each wrap generously with the garlic butter.
5. **Sear to Perfection:** Place the wraps in the skillet seam-side down. Cook for 2-3 minutes per side until golden brown, crispy, and the cheese is fully melted inside.
6. **Serve Warm:** Remove from the pan, slice in half diagonally, and serve immediately with ranch or marinara for dipping.

## Chef’s Pro Tips for Perfect Results

> **Seal the Deal:** Always place the wraps seam-side down in the skillet first. The heat will seal the tortilla shut, preventing your delicious cheesy filling from spilling out when you flip them!
