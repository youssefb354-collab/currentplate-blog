A beautifully balanced, deeply aromatic ginger chicken dinner that perfectly satisfies those savory cravings. With tender chicken breast and crisp green beans tossed in a glossy ginger-garlic sauce, this dish brings bright, punchy flavors to your weeknight rotation.

## Why You’ll Love This Recipe

- **Flavor-Packed:** The freshly grated ginger and garlic create an aromatic, punchy sauce that deeply seasons the chicken.
- **Quick & Easy:** Cooks entirely in one wok or skillet and is ready to serve in under 30 minutes.
- **Healthy & Satisfying:** Lean protein and crisp vegetables make for a nourishing, well-rounded meal.

## Kitchen Tools You’ll Need

- Large wok or deep skillet
- Cutting board & sharp chef's knife
- Microplane (for grating ginger)
- Small mixing bowl

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs Chicken Breasts (cut into bite-sized pieces)
- 2 tbsp Fresh Ginger (peeled and finely grated)
- 3 cloves Garlic (minced)
- 1/3 cup Soy Sauce (low sodium)
- 2 tbsp Honey
- 1 tbsp Rice Vinegar
- 1 tbsp Cornstarch
- 2 tbsp Sesame Oil
- 2 cups Green Beans (trimmed and halved)
- 1/4 cup Green Onions (sliced, for garnish)

## Preparation

1. **Whisk the Sauce:** In a small bowl, whisk together the soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar, cornstarch, grated ginger, and minced garlic until smooth.
2. **Sear the Chicken:** Heat one tablespoon of sesame oil in a large wok or skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken pieces and cook until browned on all sides. Remove to a plate.
3. **Stir-fry the Vegetables:** Add the remaining sesame oil to the wok. Toss in the green beans and stir-fry for 3-4 minutes until crisp-tender and brightly colored.
4. **Thicken the Glaze:** Lower the heat to medium. Give the sauce a quick stir, then pour it over the green beans, letting it bubble and thicken.
5. **Combine and Coat:** Add the chicken back into the wok. Toss everything together for 2 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and perfectly coated in the glossy glaze.
6. **Garnish and Serve:** Top with sliced green onions and serve immediately over hot steamed jasmine rice or noodles.

## Chef’s Pro Tips for Perfect Results

> **Prep Before Cooking:** Because stir-fries cook incredibly fast, have all your ingredients chopped, grated, and measured out before turning on the stove. This concept, called mise en place, ensures you won't accidentally overcook your chicken while searching for an ingredient!
