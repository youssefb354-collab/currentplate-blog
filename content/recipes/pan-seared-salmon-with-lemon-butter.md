Pan-seared salmon is a staple of fine dining, and for good reason. The combination of a crispy, caramelized crust and a tender, flaky interior is a match made in heaven. When paired with a bright and citrusy lemon butter sauce, the result is nothing short of magical. This recipe is perfect for a special occasion or a quick weeknight dinner, and can be prepared in just a few simple steps.

The key to a great pan-seared salmon is in the preparation and technique. By using high-quality ingredients and following a few simple tips, you can achieve a restaurant-quality dish in the comfort of your own home. Whether you're a seasoned chef or a culinary newcomer, this recipe is sure to impress.

## Why You’ll Love This Recipe
- **Easy to Make:** This recipe requires just a few simple ingredients and can be prepared in under 30 minutes.
- **Gluten-Free:** This dish is perfect for those with gluten intolerance or sensitivity, as it uses only gluten-free ingredients.
- **Packed with Nutrition:** Salmon is an excellent source of protein, omega-3 fatty acids, and various essential vitamins and minerals.

## Kitchen Tools You’ll Need
- Large skillet or sauté pan
- Cutting board
- Chef's knife
- Measuring cups and spoons

## Ingredients
- 4 salmon fillets (6 oz each)
- 2 lemons, juiced
- 4 tbsp unsalted butter, softened
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp dried parsley
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Preparation
1. **Prepare the Lemon Butter:** In a small bowl, mix together the softened butter, lemon juice, garlic, and parsley until well combined.
2. **Season the Salmon:** Sprinkle both sides of the salmon fillets with salt and pepper.
3. **Sear the Salmon:** Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat and add the salmon fillets, skin side up (if they have skin). Cook for 3-4 minutes, then flip and cook for an additional 3-4 minutes, or until cooked through.

## Chef’s Pro Tips for Perfect Results
> **Achieving the Perfect Sear:** To get a nice crispy crust on your salmon, make sure the skillet is hot before adding the fish. You should see a sizzle when the salmon hits the pan.
