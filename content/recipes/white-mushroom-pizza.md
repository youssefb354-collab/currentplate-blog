A rich, creamy, and deeply savory white mushroom pizza that brings gourmet pizzeria flavors straight to your kitchen. 

## Why You’ll Love This Recipe

- **Gourmet at Home:** Tastes like a fancy restaurant pizza but is incredibly easy to make.
- **No Tomato Sauce:** A garlic and herb ricotta base provides a luxurious, creamy alternative to traditional marinara.
- **Earthy Flavors:** The combination of caramelized mushrooms, garlic, and fresh thyme is absolutely intoxicating.

## Kitchen Tools You’ll Need

- Pizza stone or large baking sheet
- Large skillet
- Pizza cutter
- Small mixing bowl

## Ingredients

- 1 lb Pizza Dough (store-bought or homemade)
- 8 oz Cremini Mushrooms (sliced)
- 1/2 cup Whole Milk Ricotta Cheese
- 1.5 cups Mozzarella Cheese (shredded)
- 1/4 cup Parmesan Cheese (grated)
- 2 cloves Garlic (minced)
- 1 tbsp Olive Oil
- 1 tsp Fresh Thyme (chopped)
- 1/2 tsp Truffle Oil (optional, for drizzling)

## Preparation

1. **Preheat the Oven:** Preheat your oven to 475°F (245°C). If using a pizza stone, place it in the oven while it heats.
2. **Sauté the Mushrooms:** In a large skillet over medium-high heat, add the olive oil. Sauté the sliced mushrooms until deeply browned and their liquid has evaporated. Season with salt and pepper.
3. **Mix the White Sauce:** In a small bowl, combine the ricotta cheese, minced garlic, a pinch of salt, and half of the fresh thyme.
4. **Shape the Dough:** On a lightly floured surface, stretch or roll the pizza dough into a 12-inch circle. Place on a parchment-lined baking sheet or pizza peel.
5. **Assemble the Pizza:** Spread the garlic ricotta mixture evenly over the dough, leaving a 1-inch border. Top with the shredded mozzarella, caramelized mushrooms, and grated Parmesan.
6. **Bake and Garnish:** Bake for 12-15 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese is beautifully blistered. Remove from the oven, garnish with the remaining fresh thyme, and drizzle lightly with truffle oil before slicing.

## Chef’s Pro Tips for Perfect Results

> **Brown the Mushrooms First:** Never put raw mushrooms on a pizza! Cooking them first removes excess moisture, preventing a soggy crust and intensifying their deep, umami flavor.
