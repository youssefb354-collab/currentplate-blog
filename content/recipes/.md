The combination of red lentils, saffron, and Ethiopian spices creates a truly unique and exotic flavor profile that will transport your taste buds to the heart of Africa. These crispy lentil cakes are perfect for a special occasion or a cozy night in with friends and family. The awaze sauce, made with berbere spices and chili peppers, adds an extra layer of depth and heat to the dish.

The process of making these lentil cakes is quite therapeutic, from soaking the lentils to carefully shaping each cake and watching them transform into golden brown crisps. The aroma of saffron and spices wafting from the kitchen will tease your senses and build anticipation for the first bite.

## Why You’ll Love This Recipe
- **Vibrant Flavors:** The combination of Ethiopian spices, saffron, and lentils creates a truly unique and exciting flavor experience.
- **Crispy Texture:** The saffron crust adds a satisfying crunch to the outside of the lentil cakes, while the inside remains soft and fluffy.
- **Versatility:** These lentil cakes can be served as a main course, side dish, or even as a snack or appetizer.

## Kitchen Tools You’ll Need
- Food processor
- Non-stick skillet
- Spatula
- Cutting board
- Measuring cups and spoons

## Ingredients
- 1 cup red lentils, rinsed and drained
- 2 tablespoons saffron threads, soaked in 1 tablespoon hot water
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon ground coriander
- 1/2 teaspoon berbere spice
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 2 tablespoons awaze sauce (see note)

## Preparation
1. **Soak and Drain Lentils:** Rinse the lentils and soak them in water for at least 30 minutes. Drain and set aside.
2. **Prepare the Saffron Crust:** Mix the soaked saffron threads with the flour, cumin, coriander, berbere spice, salt, and pepper.
3. **Shape the Lentil Cakes:** Use the food processor to puree the lentils, onion, and garlic. Add the saffron crust mixture and mix until just combined. Shape into patties and coat with the saffron crust.

## Chef’s Pro Tips for Perfect Results
> **Achieving the Perfect Crust:** Make sure to not overmix the lentil mixture, and coat the cakes evenly with the saffron crust for a crispy exterior and a fluffy interior.
