Get ready to be the star of the potluck with this outrageously delicious creamy crack corn dip. Packed with sweet corn, crispy bacon, and a blend of gooey cheeses, it earns its name by being completely irresistible.

## Why You’ll Love This Recipe

- **Totally Addictive:** The combination of sweet corn, salty bacon, and spicy jalapeño is unmatched.
- **Perfect for Parties:** Easily baked in a skillet or casserole dish and served hot straight from the oven.
- **Customizable Heat:** Leave the jalapeño seeds in for an extra kick, or take them out for a milder dip.

## Kitchen Tools You’ll Need

- 10-inch cast iron skillet or baking dish
- Large mixing bowl
- Spatula or wooden spoon
- Cutting board and chef's knife

## Ingredients

- 3 cups Corn Kernels (fresh, frozen, or canned and well-drained)
- 8 oz Cream Cheese (softened)
- 1/2 cup Sour Cream
- 1/2 cup Mayonnaise
- 6 slices Bacon (cooked crispy and crumbled)
- 1 cup Cheddar Cheese (shredded)
- 1 cup Monterey Jack Cheese (shredded)
- 1 medium Jalapeño (seeded and finely diced)
- 1/4 cup Green Onions (sliced)
- 1/2 tsp Garlic Powder

## Preparation

1. **Preheat the Oven:** Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C) and lightly grease a 10-inch cast-iron skillet or an 8x8 inch baking dish.
2. **Mix the Base:** In a large bowl, mix the softened cream cheese, sour cream, mayonnaise, and garlic powder until smooth and creamy.
3. **Combine Ingredients:** Fold in the corn kernels, crumbled bacon, diced jalapeño, green onions, half of the cheddar cheese, and half of the Monterey Jack cheese.
4. **Transfer and Top:** Spread the mixture evenly into your prepared skillet or baking dish. Sprinkle the remaining cheddar and Monterey Jack cheese evenly over the top.
5. **Bake to Perfection:** Bake for 20-25 minutes until the dip is hot, bubbly around the edges, and the cheese on top is melted and lightly golden.
6. **Serve Warm:** Garnish with extra sliced green onions and a sprinkle of bacon if desired. Serve immediately with sturdy tortilla chips, crostini, or fresh vegetables!

## Chef’s Pro Tips for Perfect Results

> **Char the Corn:** For an incredible depth of flavor and a slight smokiness, pan-roast your corn kernels in a dry skillet for 5-7 minutes until they get a nice char before mixing them into the dip!
