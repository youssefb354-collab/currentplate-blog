Experience the ultimate fusion of two classic desserts with these Peach Cobbler Cinnamon Rolls. Each bite features a soft, buttery dough swirled with sweet, syrupy peaches and finished with a crunchy cobbler topping that provides the perfect textural contrast.

## Why You’ll Love This Recipe

- **The Best of Both Worlds:** Combines the comforting warmth of a cinnamon roll with the jammy, fruit-forward soul of a peach cobbler. [cite: 7]
- **Incredible Texture:** The contrast between the pillowy dough and the crisp, buttery crumble topping is addictive. [cite: 7]
- **Seasonal Showstopper:** A perfect way to showcase fresh summer peaches, though frozen peaches work beautifully year-round. [cite: 7]

## Kitchen Tools You’ll Need

- Standing mixer with a dough hook [cite: 7]
- Rolling pin [cite: 7]
- 9x13 inch baking dish [cite: 7]
- Small saucepan [cite: 7]
- Mixing bowls [cite: 7]

## Ingredients

- 4 cups All-purpose Flour
- 1 packet (2 1/4 tsp) Active Dry Yeast
- 1 cup Whole Milk (warm)
- 1/2 cup Unsalted Butter (melted)
- 1/4 cup Granulated Sugar
- 1 large Egg
- 2 cups Fresh Peaches (peeled and diced)
- 1/2 cup Brown Sugar (packed)
- 1 tbsp Ground Cinnamon
- 1/2 cup All-purpose Flour (for crumble)
- 1/4 cup Cold Butter (cubed, for crumble)
- 1 cup Powdered Sugar (for glaze)
- 2 tbsp Heavy Cream (for glaze)

## Preparation

1. **Make the Dough:** In a standing mixer, combine warm milk, yeast, and sugar. Let sit for 5 minutes. Add melted butter, egg, and flour. Knead with a dough hook for 5-7 minutes until smooth. [cite: 7]
2. **First Rise:** Place dough in a greased bowl, cover, and let rise in a warm spot for 1 hour or until doubled in size. [cite: 7]
3. **Prepare Peach Filling:** In a small saucepan, simmer diced peaches with 2 tablespoons of brown sugar over medium heat for 5 minutes until slightly softened. Drain excess liquid and let cool. [cite: 7]
4. **Roll and Fill:** Roll the dough into a large rectangle. Spread with softened butter, then sprinkle with remaining brown sugar and cinnamon. Top evenly with the peach mixture. [cite: 7]
5. **Shape and Second Rise:** Roll the dough tightly and cut into 12 even rolls. Place in a greased 9x13 dish. Cover and rise for another 30-45 minutes. [cite: 7]
6. **Bake with Crumble:** Mix the cold butter, flour, and a bit of sugar until crumbly. Sprinkle over the rolls. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 25-30 minutes until golden. [cite: 7]
7. **Glaze and Serve:** Whisk powdered sugar and heavy cream until smooth. Drizzle over the warm rolls and serve immediately. [cite: 7]

## Chef’s Pro Tips for Perfect Results

> **Don't Overcook the Peaches:** When pre-cooking the peach filling, ensure they stay slightly firm. They will continue to soften in the oven; overcooking them early will result in a mushy center rather than distinct fruit bites. [cite: 7]
