Bring the classic flavors of a BLT to your next gathering with this irresistible Bacon Tomato Dip. Creamy, savory, and bursting with fresh tomato flavor, it's the perfect crowd-pleasing appetizer that disappears in minutes.

## Why You’ll Love This Recipe

- **Crowd Pleaser:** Combining bacon and cream cheese guarantees an empty bowl at any party.
- **Quick & Easy:** Requires zero baking and comes together in just 15 minutes.
- **Versatile:** Serve it with crusty bread, sturdy crackers, or fresh vegetable sticks.

## Kitchen Tools You’ll Need

- Medium mixing bowl
- Hand mixer or sturdy whisk
- Cutting board and chef's knife
- Skillet (for cooking bacon)

## Ingredients

- 8 oz Cream Cheese (softened)
- 1/2 cup Sour Cream
- 1/2 cup Mayonnaise
- 8 slices Bacon (cooked crisp and crumbled)
- 1 cup Cherry Tomatoes (finely diced)
- 1/2 cup Cheddar Cheese (shredded)
- 1/4 cup Green Onions (thinly sliced)
- 1 tsp Garlic Powder
- 1/2 tsp Black Pepper

## Preparation

1. **Prep the Base:** In a medium mixing bowl, use a hand mixer to beat the softened cream cheese, sour cream, mayonnaise, garlic powder, and black pepper until completely smooth and fluffy.
2. **Fold in the Goodies:** Gently fold in the crumbled bacon, diced tomatoes, shredded cheddar cheese, and sliced green onions using a rubber spatula until evenly distributed.
3. **Chill and Meld:** For the best flavor, cover the bowl with plastic wrap and chill in the refrigerator for at least 1 hour to allow the savory flavors to meld together.
4. **Garnish and Serve:** Transfer the dip to a serving bowl, top with a little extra bacon and green onions, and serve chilled with crackers, crostini, or fresh veggies.

## Chef’s Pro Tips for Perfect Results

> **Seed the Tomatoes:** To prevent your dip from becoming watery as it sits, scoop out the watery seeds and pulp from your tomatoes before dicing them!
