This Thai-inspired quinoa salad is a symphony of textures and flavors, bringing a refreshing crunch and a zingy peanut kick to your table. It is the perfect balance of savory, sweet, and tangy, making it a fantastic choice for a nourishing lunch or a light dinner that keeps you satisfied.

## Why You’ll Love This Recipe

- **Nutritious and Filling:** The combination of quinoa and shredded chicken provides a double dose of protein and fiber.
- **Perfect for Meal Prep:** The flavors actually improve as it sits, making it an ideal "grab-and-go" option for the work week.
- **Vibrant and Colorful:** Loaded with fresh carrots, bell peppers, and cabbage for a meal that is as beautiful as it is delicious.

## Kitchen Tools You’ll Need

- Medium saucepan (with lid)
- Large mixing bowl
- Small jar or mixing bowl (for dressing)
- Whisk
- Sharp chef's knife

## Ingredients

- 1 cup Quinoa (rinsed)
- 2 cups Water or Chicken Broth
- 2 cups Cooked Chicken (shredded or diced)
- 1 cup Red Bell Pepper (thinly sliced)
- 1 cup Carrots (shredded)
- 1/2 cup Red Cabbage (thinly sliced)
- 1/4 cup Fresh Cilantro (chopped)
- 3 tbsp Creamy Peanut Butter
- 2 tbsp Soy Sauce (low sodium)
- 1 tbsp Lime Juice (freshly squeezed)
- 1 tsp Honey or Agave
- 1/2 tsp Fresh Ginger (finely grated)
- 1/4 cup Roasted Peanuts (chopped, for garnish)

## Preparation

1. **Cook the Quinoa:** In a medium saucepan, combine the rinsed quinoa and water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes or until liquid is absorbed. Remove from heat and fluff with a fork; let it cool completely.
2. **Whisk the Dressing:** In a small bowl or jar, combine the peanut butter, soy sauce, lime juice, honey, and grated ginger. Whisk until smooth and creamy. If it’s too thick, add a teaspoon of warm water to reach your desired consistency.
3. **Assemble the Salad:** In a large mixing bowl, combine the cooled quinoa, shredded chicken, bell peppers, carrots, and cabbage.
4. **Dress and Toss:** Pour the peanut dressing over the salad and toss thoroughly to ensure every ingredient is well-coated.
5. **Garnish and Chill:** Fold in the fresh cilantro and top with chopped roasted peanuts. For the best flavor, refrigerate for at least 20 minutes before serving to allow the dressing to meld with the ingredients.

## Chef’s Pro Tips for Perfect Results

> **Toast the Quinoa:** For a deeper, nuttier flavor profile, toast the dry quinoa in the saucepan over medium heat for 2-3 minutes until it smells fragrant and slightly nutty before adding the cooking liquid.
